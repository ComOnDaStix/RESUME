About

Based in

Milwaukee, WI · USA

Experience

Working since 2019

Specialization

Editorial · Portrait · Lifestyle · Wedding · Video

I'm Com, a self-taught photographer and videographer based in Milwaukee. I make pictures and films for brands and people I believe in: campaigns, editorial, lookbooks, lifestyle, and weddings.

I helped scale Unfinished Legacy from a Milwaukee idea into a global clothing brand, and helped grow its flagship store in the Third Ward into a fixture of the city. I've worked with the Brewers, the Bucks, the Milwaukee Art Museum, and lensed a campaign for a candidate running for governor.

I've spent years studying what builds brands and testing what I learn against real work, which means I show up with more than a camera. I understand the market, the audience, and how supply and demand actually move a brand forward.

I work end to end: concept, capture, delivery. The work ships polished, on brand, and on time. If it's worth shooting, I want to see it.
